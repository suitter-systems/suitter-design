# Suitter design pages

## Themes and room-state colours

**Live page:** https://suitter-systems.github.io/suitter-design/

The six themes of Suitter, a property management system for hotels and resorts, shown on today's room status board in Arabic, the product's first language. A theme changes only the shell: the top bar, the menu, the buttons and links, the ground, the fonts, the corners and the density. The screen's layout and the unit-state colours stay the same in every theme.

- **Unit-state colours** are a setting of their own, with two sets: "standard" (the default for every hotel) and "red for occupied". Every theme works with both sets; only the colours change between them, never the words or the icons.
- **Contrast is checked on the page** for every theme with every set: text 4.5:1, borders, housekeeping marks and the focus ring 3:1 (WCAG 2.1, 1.4.3 and 1.4.11).
- **Oasis (واحة)** is the theme the product uses. The six themes are the visual directions reviewed on 4 Oct 2026, with the 5 Oct review's changes.
- Names and figures on the page are generated examples, not guest data. Only the state legend works: it filters the board.
- The earlier page of six directions on the reception screen is in this repository's history (commit e400c95).

## Front desk screens

**Live pages:** https://suitter-systems.github.io/suitter-design/front-desk/

Static copies of the front desk prototype's screens (version 36, 5 Oct 2026) in the Oasis look: today's arrivals, departures and in-house guests, check-in, the room timeline and today's room status. Moving between the screens works; the other controls do nothing. Names and figures are generated examples, not guest data.

## Choosing the look

**Live page:** https://suitter-systems.github.io/suitter-design/choose-the-look/

Four looks for the front desk, each with its own navigation and screen layout, shown on four screens: today's arrivals, check-in, the room timeline and today's room status. All four show the same example day at the same moment. A viewer picks a look for each screen, then one look overall; the picks stay in that browser, and the page gives a summary to copy and send. Names and figures are generated examples, not guest data.
